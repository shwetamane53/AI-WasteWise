PLASTIC WASTE

Examples:
Plastic bottles, plastic containers, plastic packaging.

Category:
Plastic Waste.

Recommended Action:
Clean suitable plastic items and place them in the appropriate recyclable waste collection.

Recycling:
Suitable plastic items can be recycled through authorized collection systems.

Safety:
Keep plastic waste away from fire and do not burn plastic.


BATTERY WASTE

Examples:
AA batteries, AAA batteries, rechargeable batteries, lithium-ion batteries.

Category:
Hazardous / E-Waste.

Recommended Action:
Take batteries to an authorized battery or e-waste collection point.

Recycling:
Batteries should be handled through proper recycling facilities.

Safety:
Do not burn, puncture, crush, or open batteries.


E-WASTE

Examples:
Mobile phones, chargers, computers, keyboards, electronic devices.

Category:
E-Waste.

Recommended Action:
Take electronic items to an authorized e-waste collection center.

Recycling:
Electronic components can be recovered through proper e-waste recycling.

Safety:
Do not burn or dismantle electronic devices.


ORGANIC WASTE

Examples:
Food waste, vegetable peels, fruit waste, banana peels.

Category:
Organic Waste.

Recommended Action:
Put organic waste in the appropriate wet/organic waste collection.

Recycling:
Organic waste can be composted where suitable.

Safety:
Keep organic waste separate from electronic and hazardous waste.


PAPER WASTE

Examples:
Newspaper, paper, cardboard, notebooks.

Category:
Paper Waste.

Recommended Action:
Keep paper clean and dry and place it in recyclable waste collection.

Recycling:
Paper and cardboard can usually be recycled.

Safety:
Keep paper dry and free from food contamination.


TEXTILE WASTE

Examples:
Old clothes, shirts, jeans, dresses, garments, fabric.

Category:
Textile Waste.

Recommended Action:
Donate wearable clothes or send unusable textiles to an appropriate textile recycling facility.

Recycling:
Old clothes can be reused, repaired, upcycled, or recycled into new textile products.

Safety:
Do not burn clothes, especially synthetic fabrics, because they may release harmful fumes.
